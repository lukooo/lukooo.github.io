01. About me

I'm Mgr. Zdenka Hudáková, a graphic designer working under the brand CreativePaper. For over 12 years I've focused on graphic design specializing in book design — designing book and e-book covers and typesetting (DTP) book interiors for publishers from Slovakia, the Czech Republic and abroad. Besides book design, I also create other print and digital graphics — posters, flyers, wedding announcements, invitations and greeting cards.
