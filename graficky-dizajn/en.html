04. Frequently asked questions

How long does a book cover design take?

It depends on the scope of the brief and the number of revisions, usually a few days to two weeks from brief to final version.

Do you also do the typesetting (DTP) for the whole book, or just the cover?

Both — I design the cover as well as complete interior typesetting, ready for print and digital distribution.

Can you prepare a book in the correct format for Amazon KDP (self-publishing)?

Yes, I can prepare both the cover and interior layout to meet Amazon KDP (Kindle Direct Publishing) specifications for print and e-books, so you can upload and publish your book directly.

Do you work with international clients and publishers?

Yes, I have experience working with international publishers, and communication also happens in English.

How does the collaboration and file handover work?

We communicate online, exchanging files and feedback electronically, so working together works regardless of distance.

Do you also do graphics outside of book design, such as wedding announcements?

Yes, I also work on wedding announcements, invitations, posters, flyers and other promotional graphics.

Where can I see more of your work?

You can find the full portfolio at creativepaper.sk or in the gallery on navolnenoze.cz — links are also in the Selected work section above.
