01. About the company

M.SJ s.r.o. is an accounting and tax office based in Prague 9 – Letňany. The company was founded in 2015 and has been run from the start by two managing directors with years of experience in accounting and tax. We specialize in comprehensive bookkeeping for small and medium-sized businesses, sole traders and non-profit organizations — from bookkeeping, through payroll, to tax returns and advisory services.
